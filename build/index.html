Principles

Analyze everything.

Waste not, want not. Be prepared to capture every ounce of information that you can from the very beginning. You can't analyze what you don't know.

Be agile.

Being able to develop quickly and easily is the key to launching a brilliant product. Waste time and you risk cutting corners and disappointing users.

Communicate often.

Your users want to hear from you. Speak clearly and be rewarded with their attention.

Focus on product.

What matters? Your product. Don't get caught re-inventing the wheel. Every hour you waste trying to catch-up is another hour you fall farther behind.

Performance matters.

Your user's time is precious. Delight them with speed and they'll reward you with their attention.

Stay flexible.

Modern businesses have complex and mercurial needs. You need to be able to adapt to change and leverage every advantage you can.

Scale infinitely.

Success can catch you by surprise when you least expect it. Don't just survive a flood of new interest, flourish in it.

Validate your assumptions.

Think you have a good idea? Prove it. Test every hypothesis. Nobody gets it right the first time. Every idea needs validation.
