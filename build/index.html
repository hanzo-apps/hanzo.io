Principles

Analyze

Waste not, want not. Be prepared to capture every ounce of information that you can from the very beginning. You can't analyze what you don't know.

Validate

Think you have a good idea? Prove it. Test every hypothesis. Nobody gets it right the first time. Every idea needs validation.

Agility

Being able to develop quickly and easily is the key to launching a brilliant product. Waste time and you risk cutting corners and disappointing users.

Performance

Your user's time is precious. Delight them with speed and respect their time and they'll reward you with their attention.

Flexibility

Modern businesses have complex needs that are always changing. You need to be able to adapt to changing conditions and leverage every advantage you can.

Focus

Focus on what matters: your product. Don't get caught up in minutae and un-important details. Every hour you waste on something else is an hour you fall further behind.

Communicate

Your users want to hear from you. Speak clearly and be rewarded with their attention.
